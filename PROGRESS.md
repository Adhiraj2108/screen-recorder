**Audio feature (added 16 November 2026):** Clean up the narration without taking anything away. The owner asked for less background noise and room echo, plus small safe quality gains. ScreenCaptureKit has no noise or echo setting for its microphone capture, so the app now cleans the saved file right after recording (`VoiceCleanup.swift`): Apple's voice isolation (high-quality voice model), an 80 Hz rumble filter, a 2 dB level boost (about 25% louder) with a peak limiter 1 dB below full scale, and AAC at 96 kbps per channel instead of about 53. The video is copied untouched, the cleaned audio goes back at the original start time with the isolation delay removed, and the original file is kept if any step fails. The narration is decoded on the video's timeline from time zero, so ScreenCaptureKit's lead-in offset (about 0.38 s) becomes silence and sync holds. A "Clean up voice" switch (on by default) lets the owner compare. Video stays H.264 at 30 fps.

**Optional system audio (started 17 November 2026):** A "Record Mac sound" switch records what the Mac is playing (a video, a prototype's sound) alongside the voice, using ScreenCaptureKit (this app's own sounds are excluded). Real finding from the owner's first Mac test: macOS mixes the Mac sound and the microphone into one stereo audio track while recording, with no separate voice track. I first assumed two tracks and built a mixing step; the real file showed one, so that step was removed. Consequence: voice cleanup needs the voice on its own, so it is not applied while Mac sound is on, and the "Clean up voice" switch greys out with a note. Video stays H.264 at 30 fps, and recordings with the switch off are unchanged (cleanup works as before). Headphones are advised, since speakers would be heard by the mic as well as captured directly. If cleaned voice plus Mac sound is wanted later, it needs a different capture design that keeps the sources separate (own audio writers instead of Apple's recording output); that is on the later list and would need sync re-testing.

**Auto-upload, link, clean-up and delete (Milestone 4, started 17 November 2026):** The owner asked for four changes to the Mac app (app only, no change to the live site). (1) A finished recording uploads by itself; recordings made during an upload wait in line. (2) Once the upload is confirmed, the link is made and copied. (3) The local recording moves to the Trash about 5 days after the upload is confirmed. (4) A Delete button removes the uploaded video online, with an option to move the recording to the Trash as well. Both automatic behaviours are visible switches in the Share area (on by default), and a Recent uploads list shows each video's local status and Trash date. Safety rules (`UploadLibrary.swift`): an upload counts as confirmed only when the server reports it ready and holding exactly the number of bytes sent; only confirmed uploads are ever cleaned up; just before moving a file the server is asked again, and the file stays if the server can't be reached, the video was deleted online, or the file changed since upload; files go to the Trash, never straight to deletion. The owner key stays in the Keychain.

**Hosting decision (17 November 2026):** The owner chose free Cloudflare R2 storage over paid Stream, on the condition that clients get a Loom-like experience (open a link in any browser or phone, press play, fullscreen, speed, seeking). Cloudflare's R2 page (updated 30 Oct 2026) lists a free tier of 10 GB-month storage, 1 million write and 10 million read operations a month, and no charge for data sent to viewers; beyond that $0.015 per GB-month. Real recordings run about 0.8 Mbps (about 6 MB a minute for simple windows), so about 70 to 150 ten-minute videos fit. What differs from Stream, honestly: no automatic lower-quality versions, so a very slow connection may buffer on long videos (the viewer always gets the full-quality file, which also keeps dense Figma text sharp); the player is the browser's own with our speed buttons. Stream can be swapped in later for chosen videos without redoing the owner page or share links. Design: private bucket with no public address; the Worker checks the share link on every request; 30-minute signed playback addresses tied to one share link (so revoking works at once); Range support for seeking; delete removes the stored file. Still to do for fast starts: the app's saved MP4s have their index at the end, so the app needs a one-line "optimise for streaming" setting (done in step 3 with the Upload button).
